### New
- Agents and Workstreams now have their own tabs on iPhone
- Agent chats keep a quiet unread marker after you leave and relaunch, so you always know what's new.
- Waiting on You cards can now carry up to four screenshots as proof, viewable right from the card.
- HQ can now learn your recurring approval choices and start applying them automatically, with a running log you can review or undo anytime.
- Filed artifacts (images and web snapshots) now show up in a shelf you can browse, open, discuss, or delete.

### Improved
- Updates now install through a simpler, more native update window.
- HQ Remote's buttons, tabs, and toggles are now a calm black and white instead of blue, so red only appears when something truly needs attention.
- The artifact shelf and Workstreams list got a cleaner, more native look with clearer swipe actions.
- Large lists (reviews, sidebars, project history) now load noticeably faster as they grow.
- Long chat transcripts scroll more smoothly and stay anchored in place while new messages load.

### Fixed
- Skill instructions no longer flood the chat as a giant wall of text — they collapse into a short note, like other system messages.
- Artifact viewers now match your light or dark mode setting instead of forcing dark.
- Messages to an agent that's asleep or restarting now wait until it's actually ready, instead of getting lost.
- Reopening a project no longer occasionally starts a duplicate chat thread.
